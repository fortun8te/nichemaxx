# nichemaxx

A Claude skill that is a *way of seeing*.

Not a tool. Not a TikTok-brief generator. A taste — dark literary, dangerous calm, cleanboy — that an agent carries in its bones, so it can think alongside someone who has the feeling but not the references yet.

## What it's for

You have moods. Fragments. A track stuck in your head. Something half-formed you can't make coherent. What you don't have is the reference library — the specific scene, the specific track, the photographer's specific series — that turns a vague feeling into one clear, niche thing.

That's the gap this closes. You bring the mess; it hands you the world, then makes it makeable.

## How to use it

```
/nichemaxx i keep thinking about driving at night and feeling nothing, but not in a bad way
/nichemaxx                      → it gives you something from the brain, unprompted
/nichemaxx check [an idea]      → honest read: does this belong, and how to push it
```

It doesn't fill in a form. It distills what you're reaching for, hands you references you couldn't have found alone, tells you how to build it, and names the one move that would make it basic.

## The one rule

Nothing with energy. Everything with texture. Make it for the people who already know.

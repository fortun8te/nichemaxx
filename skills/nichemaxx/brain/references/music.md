## THE SONIC RULE

**Nothing with energy. Everything with texture.**

No drops. No pop hooks. Tempos that feel slightly slower than normal. Emotionally legible but never obvious. The track selection IS the personality — Foo Fighters for a moodboard is a choice, Minnie Riperton for a flatlay is a choice, Enigma for a Supreme waistband video is a massive choice.

## DEEP TRACK ANALYSIS

### Sade — "Like A Tattoo (Skep's Jungle Edit)" [@ilierk]
The original "Like A Tattoo" (1992, *Love Deluxe*) is already sophisticated. Skep's jungle edit adds trip-hop percussion without losing the warmth. Finding this edit requires knowing it exists — it's not prominent on streaming. This signals: I've gone deeper than Spotify playlists. Highest musical IQ in the 36-video dataset.

### fakemink — "night, blooming jasmine" [@ryynvyi, @mediaarchivesss]
Cloud rap / deep house hybrid. Title quotes David Lynch's gravestone inscription. fakemink knew about the gravestone before Lynch died in January 2025 — already using it as a reference. Two accounts, same track, same caption, same week. Community password: if you understand why, you're in.

### Nettspend — "Still Standing" [@defjuj]
Nu-jerk micro-genre: staggered snares, pitched-up vocals, time stutters, 808s slightly off-beat on purpose. Nettspend born 2007, making music at 17 about what it feels like to be 17 and watched. "Still Standing" is about surviving something without knowing what. *Early Life Crisis* mixtape title = the genre's thesis.

### George Clanton / ESPRIT 空想 — "Warmpop" [@elevationovation]
Vaporwave = nostalgia for futures that never arrived. Most vaporwave is ironic. George Clanton is sincere vaporwave — he actually means it. "Warmpop" wants to be happy and almost succeeds. This community doesn't do irony. The Clanton choice makes sense precisely because it's real.

### mikal — "lucki xcx" [@kanetheicon]
Lucki = Chicago, dissociative trap, designed to soundtrack going nowhere. Charli XCX brat era = glitchy pop, hook buried in chaos. The blend: numb with a melody underneath. For a video that's just a face saying "beep beep" — exact.

### Minnie Riperton — "Les Fleurs" [@pintfindss11]
1970 soul. Five-octave voice used gently. About flowers, love, the physical world — embarrassingly sincere by current standards. The dataset's only uncomplicated joy. Peaches and tabbouleh flatlay needs exactly this.

### Enigma — "The Rivers Of Belief (Extended)" [@secretyoullneverknowuh]
Gregorian chant, ambient new age, deep bass, 1990. Sensuality + spirituality. Using this over a Supreme waistband / Parisian apartment moodboard is either completely earnest or the most self-aware move in the dataset. The community probably doesn't register the irony. That's correct — they mean it.

### Foo Fighters — "Everlong" [@sallyarchives]
1997 alt-rock. Emotionally raw. Over images of a BMW cafe racer and Mount Fuji from a suburban rooftop, this adds genuine ache to a flat aspiration moodboard. The community absorbs Foo Fighters because the feeling is right even if the genre is unexpected.

### Solange — "Losing You" [@juliancoolians]
2012 minimal R&B. Warmer and quieter than her sister. About a relationship ending but treated with lightness that makes the sadness bearable. Over a car window selfie with autumn blurring past — exact. The juliancoolians creator has good ears.

## WHO IS THIS PERSON

**Age:** 16–22. The sweet spot is 17–19 — old enough to have taste, young enough to be building it.  
**Gender:** Predominantly male. The community centers on a masculine self-image, but the gaze is frequently androgynous or queer-adjacent.  
**Location:** European-coded — French, Belgian, Dutch, Italian, Scandinavian. Some LA adjacency (Chrome Hearts, beach, Coachella). The aesthetic fluency is distinctly Continental.  
**Class signal:** Aspiring upper-middle. Not actually rich — but so deeply fluent in the visual language of wealth and taste that it doesn't matter. The apartment in @brennorks costs what it costs, but the way he films it is free.  
**Device:** iPhone (probably older), AirPods or wired earbuds. Edits in CapCut, occasionally After Effects for the technical ceiling (@dupeoctobr).  
**Platform behavior:** Consumer much more than creator. Saves compulsively. The #targetaudience tag is their most honest moment: they are calling themselves out for being the audience. They don't want reach. They want recognition.

---

### What he looks like

Lean. Not gym-lean — just thin, the way people are when they eat well and move through the world without urgency. Supreme waistband visible above low-rise trousers, two belts stacked. One piece of jewelry: baroque cross or chunky ring, or nothing. Hair intentional but not styled-looking. Wears the same three colors all year.

**His face — the physical ideal this community orbits:** Soft jaw. High cheekbones. Features that read clean rather than heavy. The ideal face in this community has a detectable feminine quality — it's not effeminate, it's *refined*. Think young Alain Delon, which the community literally posts as aspirational content: strong but not aggressive, defined but not rough. The face photographs perfectly from every angle because the structure is symmetrical and the proportions are closer to a model than a typical guy.

This is the Delon effect: he was considered one of the most beautiful men alive, and part of what made it work was that his beauty had a quality usually attributed to women — regularity of feature, softness of skin, the jaw that's defined but never brutal. The community is drawn to this specific register of masculinity. Not the body-builder, not the rough-hewn — the face that's almost too good-looking.

**Who actually posts themselves in this community:** @kanetheicon posts his own face and it *functions* as aesthetic content — soft, feminine-boned, curly dark hair — without him explicitly positioning himself as aesthetic content. @jakewarden posts himself explicitly as "femboy-chella" and the community receives it. @juliancoolians has a face that could genuinely be read either way. @brennorks is the one masculine exception — darker, firmer features, Brazilian physicality — but even he presents through dancing rather than posturing.

**The sexuality read of the community:** Aesthetically bisexual. Practically probably majority straight, but operating in a space where queer aesthetics, queer film references (CMBYN), and appreciation of the soft male face are entirely normalized. Nobody explains it. Nobody has to. The CMBYN edit and the Delon post and the "femboy-chella" video all live in the same feed and nobody blinks. The androgyny is the point.

## THE VISUAL GRAMMAR

These are the editing rules the community follows. Not trends — a shared grammar.

**Letterbox crop** — black bars top and bottom. Makes everything feel like cinema. Also signals: this is not casual content, this is framed.

**Eye close-up as opener** — macke.v3 does this on most videos. Eyes in extreme close-up before you see anything else. Blue-green eyes, high contrast, slightly overexposed skin. Forces intimacy before context.

**Match cut on shape / motion** — cutting between two things that share geometry or movement direction. Not standard editing — requires actual craft or at least a strong eye. @dupeoctobr's After Effects Shining recreation is the technical ceiling of this.

**Fade to black** — not cut to black. Fade. Gives everything a sense of ending rather than stopping.

**Found / archival footage** — sourcing from real things (Elle backstage VHS, Riefenstahl's Olympia, CMBYN, Le Samourai, Lynch interviews) rather than creating. The community's aesthetics depend on knowing where to look, not how to produce.

**No text, or one word, or a fragment** — "beep beep." "te amo." "Life!" The incomplete quote. The non-sentence. If you need more text than that, the image isn't working.

**Sound-first editing** — the track is chosen before the visuals. The edit serves the music's tempo and emotional arc, not the other way around. This is why the audio choices matter so much.

## THE UNCOMFORTABLE CONTEXT

The cleanboy ideal — lean, European-coded, disciplined, beautiful, emotionally controlled — has an ideological shadow.

@vrillence documents this explicitly: Riefenstahl's Olympia (specifically made to aestheticize the male body for the 1936 Berlin Olympics, Nazi Germany's propaganda showcase) + Arno Breker (who sculpted the idealized Aryan body for public Nazi monuments) + #willezurmacht (the Nietzsche quote weaponized by Nazi appropriation). These sit alongside #aesthetic and #targetaudience.

Meanwhile, @3nocten posts queer longing (CMBYN). @jakewarden posts androgynous Coachella femininity. @maniaaauser posts intimate female self-portraits.

These coexist in the same TikTok feed. The community doesn't organize around the ideology — but it doesn't reject it either. The ambiguity is deliberate: the aesthetic appeals broadly; the ideology is deniable as "just references."

**Any creator making content for or adjacent to this community needs to understand this clearly before they start.**

## YOUR CREATIVE TAKEAWAY

**What they do well:** Sonic restraint. Letterbox. Slow cuts. Fade to black. Match cut as primary technique. Archival sourcing. Reference depth. The "content for people who already know" energy. The audio selection is genuinely extraordinary — whoever is listening to Sade trip-hop edits, fakemink, George Clanton, Solange, and Minnie Riperton in the same feed has real taste.

**What they don't do:** Energy. Warmth (mostly). Humor. Explaining themselves. Female voices (mostly). Joy (mostly). Irony. Moving forward.

**Your edge over this community:** Your creative brief already goes further — androgyny, brat rawness, Lynch surrealism, architecture as danger. They do cleanboy well. You can do cleanboy *plus everything it's missing.* That's the harder and more interesting thing. The question is whether you can bring the sonic restraint (because they have genuinely good ears) while adding the thing they're afraid of: actually feeling it.

The @kanetheicon video is the accidental key. The most stripped-back video in the dataset — one face, one track, zero production — is also the most honest. If your content can have that quality of presence while also having the visual craft of macke.v3 and the audio taste of the @ilierk Sade choice, you're already doing something this community hasn't seen yet.

## THE ICP (Ideal Community Profile)

**Age:** 16–22.  
**Gender:** Male, majority.  
**Location:** European-coded — French, Belgian, Dutch dominant. Some LA adjacency (Chrome Hearts cluster).  
**Class signal:** Aspiring upper-middle. Not actually rich, but deeply fluent in the visual language of wealth and taste.  
**Device:** iPhone, probably older model. Edits in CapCut or occasionally After Effects.  
**Platform behavior:** Consumer more than creator. Saves everything. #targetaudience = they ARE the audience, calling themselves out.

### What he looks like
Lean. Supreme waistband visible above low-rise trousers. One piece of jewelry — either baroque (ring, cross) or nothing. Hair intentional but not styled-looking. Owns one genuinely old thing (a book, a record, a coat). Wears the same three colors in rotation.

### What he does
Watches Melville films. Reads but won't tell you what. Meditates or at least uses the language of meditation. Drinks matcha positioned as a design object. Knows who Alain Delon is without being told to care. Has a playlist nobody else has heard. Takes photos on a camera that makes everything look 1974. Gym, but not the kind with mirrors.

### What he wants
To be legible to exactly the right people and invisible to everyone else. Not fame — *recognition*. The feeling of being seen by someone who gets it. The comment that says "how did you find this."

### What he fears
Being basic. Being try-hard. Being too online. (He is very online.)

### His TikTok behavior
Doesn't make talking videos. Makes edits. Sources archival footage, runway clips, film stills. Posts with minimal caption — either no text, a single word, or a quote nobody will Google. Uses letterbox. Fades to black. The account bio is either empty or a single line from a film he won't name.

## THE UNCOMFORTABLE CONTEXT

@vrillence uses #willezurmacht (Nietzsche/Nazi appropriation), #breker (Arno Breker — Hitler's official sculptor), and Leni Riefenstahl's *Olympia* alongside #targetaudience. This is documented as part of the "Aryan Classic TikTok" movement.

The community doesn't organize around this but doesn't reject it either. The cleanboy ideal — lean, European-coded, disciplined, beautiful — has an ideological shadow some members lean into deliberately. Queer longing (CMBYN) and proto-fascist aesthetics (Breker's idealized male body) coexist through deliberate ambiguity.

**Know this before you make content for or adjacent to this community.**

### What the data says

The content that performs is emotionally accessible: solitude as peace, grief that lifts, queer longing, diary vulnerability. Same aesthetic register, slightly more human.

### @kanetheicon — the zero-cut signal
"beep beep" — 156K views, 19.5% like rate, 137 comments on 156K views.
Zero cuts. Zero editing. Dashboard POV night drive, 15 seconds, lo-fi vaporwave.
The comment-to-view ratio is high — people need to respond to this. They recognize themselves in it.
**What this means:** in a community of highly crafted edits, the most authentic thing — someone just driving alone at night and recording it — performs as well as careful construction. The feeling is the point. Craft is one path to the feeling. Authenticity is another.

### @dent_13 — BPM: 0
No music. Pure ambient drone, field recording, silence. 24% like rate. 79K views.
Silence works. The community can sit with nothing.

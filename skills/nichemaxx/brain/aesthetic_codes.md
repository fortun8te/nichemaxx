# AESTHETIC CODES

## When to Invoke Each Code
These seven codes represent distinct visual and sonic territories. Mix them — they coexist in a single frame via layering, contradiction, and careful pacing. Each code has a specific visual signature, color temperature, and emotional register. Invoke by shooting the technical specs listed below; the viewer will recognize the code through accumulated detail, not explanation.

---

## ENIGMA / ANDROGYNOUS

**Invoke when:** The identity of the figure is the secret. The audience is building mythology in the comment section.

**Visual Grammar:**
- Monochromatic dark neutrals, structural silhouettes, nothing casually gendered
- Camera on hands, jawlines, fabric — body parts that are ambiguous
- Eye contact rare and brief — when it happens, cut immediately after
- Lighting: side light or backlight, never front-lit against the face
- Clothing: tailored silhouettes (YSL, CDG, Chrome Hearts) with no readable gender markers

**Sonic Palette:**
- Cigarettes After Sex, Boy Harsher, Ethel Cain, Øneheart
- 55-72 BPM, low sustained hum underneath
- Barely-audible industrial ambient layered under primary track

**Technical Specs:**
- Color: Navy, near-black, unbleached stone (#C8B89A) only
- Skin: Overexposed highlights, dark shadows, no middle tone
- Shot duration: 8-12 seconds per clip
- Texture mandatory: wool, linen, velvet, water surface — no smooth navy

**The Rule:** The withholding of identity IS the content. Never show the face straight-on. Never explain who they are.

## DAVID LYNCH

**Invoke when:** The ordinary has been poisoned. The wrongness is the entire point.

**Visual Grammar:**
- The cozy thing that has been poisoned. Not just dark — the specific wrongness of the ordinary
- Practical lighting in darkness: two sources only, rest in shadow
- The corridor. The threshold. The curtain that moves with nobody near it
- Hold shots until discomfort arrives, then hold longer
- Sodium-lamp orange, electric blue, or red velvet under tungsten

**Sonic Palette:**
- Angelo Badalamenti's blueprint: 55-72 BPM, heavy reverb, low sustained hum underneath
- Sound design: layer barely-audible industrial ambient under music. The HVAC hum of a specific room
- Silence with room tone (air conditioning, electrical buzz, carpet static)

**Technical Specs:**
- Colors: Red velvet / deep crimson (#C41E3A) + electric blue (#0066FF). These two colors together = full Lynch equation
- Shadows: deep and textured, never flat black
- Lighting placement: one warm, one cool source. Rest in shadow
- Camera movement: imperceptible creeping zoom or completely static
- Duration: 10-15 seconds minimum per shot — the discomfort must have time to arrive

**The Rule:** Beauty here is the last moment before something breaks. Never resolve the wrongness. Let it sit.

## FAKEMINK / UNDERGROUND RAP

**Invoke when:** Luxury is dirty. History is compressed. The image itself is an artifact.

**Visual Grammar:**
- "The mink is the luxury part; the fake is the dirty." Dirty luxury.
- Deep-fried Instagram aesthetic: over-compressed, blurry, early 2010s internet feel
- Archive Supreme, CDG, Hedi Slimane slim silhouettes, unexplained archival references
- Director LAUZZA's approach: DIY-but-deliberate. Chaotic energy that is architected, not accidental
- High-flash night photography: harsh on-camera flash, pitch-black backgrounds
- Frutiger Aero/Metro for digital texture: Windows XP softness, blue gradients, Y2K warmth

**Sonic Palette:**
- Cloud rap meets deep house (fakemink reference: suspended time, barely-there production)
- 60-80 BPM, pitched-up vocals, staggered snares, time stutters
- Lo-fi compression as aesthetic choice, not accident — the voice sits in blur

**Technical Specs:**
- Color: Purple/blue in shadows, deep-fried compression creating color shift
- ISO: Pushed high (1600+) intentionally — grain is texture, not failure
- Contrast: Blown highlights on one figure, pitch-black background behind
- Font: Frutiger Aero, soft gradients, Y2K UI elements
- Aspect ratio: 2.35:1 within 9:16, letterbox sacred

**The Rule:** Archival sources take precedence. The compression is intentional. The image looks like it arrived from the early internet on purpose.

## BRAT

**Invoke when:** The confession matters more than the composition. Rawness is deliberate.

**Visual Grammar:**
- Font: Arial Narrow, lowercase, 5-7px Gaussian blur, 150dpi, tight tracking
- Photography: direct flash, club spaces, motion blur, grain, no posed energy
- Color: #8ACE00 as a single neon accent — not a palette, a punch
- The text says something confessional or direct — it doesn't just mimic the look
- Authentic use: lean into New Rave / club photography lineage, not the meme layer

**Sonic Palette:**
- Hyperpop energy (140+ BPM) or minimal deconstructed (60-80 BPM)
- If fast: distorted 808s, glitchy production, staggered rhythm
- If slow: acid textures, warped instruments, vocal processing
- Always: unapologetic production choices, no smoothing

**Technical Specs:**
- Text placement: random grid, overlapping, sometimes cut off
- Blur amount: 5-7px exactly — enough to soften, not obliterate
- One neon accent: #8ACE00 appears exactly once per frame or not at all
- Color balance: everything else is dark (navy, black) to let acid pop
- Font size: 5-8px for body text, never larger
- Filter: slightly over-compressed JPEG artifacts acceptable

**The Rule:** The text is a confession. Direct, unfiltered. The look serves the truth, not the reverse.

## JAMES BOND / COOL MASCULINE ENIGMA

**Invoke when:** The outsider walks into the room and owns it through refusal to perform.

**Visual Grammar:**
- Casino Royale (2006) as primary reference: beautiful damage, wet linen, navy polo
- Skyfall's palette: midnight navy Tom Ford tuxedo, rain-soaked grey
- Casino color palette: green baize + amber tungsten + cream chips + oxblood trim
- The arriving shot: walk into frame from behind. Camera doesn't chase. It waits
- Getting ready ritual: slow shots of clothing being put on, watch being fastened. No voiceover
- The androgynous Bond: tuxedo with no shirt underneath. The masculine silhouette destabilized

**Sonic Palette:**
- Neo-spy jazz: 55-72 BPM, minor key, vibraphone shimmer, Melody Gardot territory
- Minimalist arrangement: one instrument line clear, rest in shadow
- Silence used as tension: music drops, only footsteps or breathing
- No percussion — rhythm comes from strings or sustained hum

**Technical Specs:**
- Colors: Navy (#1C2340, #0A1628), oxblood, green baize (#165E4F), cream
- Fabrics: wet linen, cashmere, wool, silk — shot to show texture
- Jewelry: minimal and precise (watch, single ring, cufflinks) — these are the only bright elements
- Lighting: warm amber tungsten against cool rain/night exterior
- Duration: 6-10 seconds per clip; getting-ready scenes are the longest (12-15 sec)
- Movement: slow, deliberate, economical. No wasted motion

**The Rule:** Competence is sexier than performance. Never chase the camera. Let the camera wait for you.

## ARCHITECTURE

**Invoke when:** The building is the character. The human is scale, not subject.

**Visual Grammar:**
- Buildings are not subjects — they are environments. The camera is searching, not documenting
- Brutalism for danger, Haussmann Paris for inheritance, Japanese minimalism for the sacred
- The upward reveal (start at ground, tilt up slowly), the symmetry lock, the compression pull
- Long lens: makes Haussmann facades stack into graphic abstraction
- One figure, small in frame, as scale reference only. Architecture dominates
- Sound design: the actual ambient of the building. Specific echo of a concrete corridor

**Sonic Palette:**
- Silence with spatial audio: the acoustic character of the building itself
- Low drone: 40-60 Hz sine wave, barely perceptible, creates dread
- Footsteps on specific surfaces: metal, marble, concrete, wood — each has a signature
- Whispered French conversation half-heard, not subtitled

**Technical Specs:**
- Framing: human in lower third or far background (5-20% of frame)
- Lens: 50mm or longer for flattening, stacking planes
- Lighting: one strong directional source (window, corridor end point)
- Color: desaturated for brutalism; warm stone tones for Haussmann; cool minimalism for sacred
- Camera movement: imperceptible creep or complete stillness; no pans
- Duration: 8-14 seconds per shot; let the geometry read

**The Rule:** The building has waited longer than you. Let it dominate the frame completely.

## EURO SUMMER (ELEVATED)

**Invoke when:** The moment is ordinary, but the light is transcendent. Nothing is chased. Everything is found.

**Visual Grammar:**
- Sam Youkilis is the template: ordinary over iconic, people not objects, stillness not panning
- No drone. No golden hour chasing. Shoot at 10pm when the stone holds heat
- Hold individual clips 6-12 seconds. The edit breathes
- Film at blue hour: disable auto white balance, set to Tungsten, underexpose 1/3 stop
- The figure is ambiguous in importance — sometimes foreground, sometimes background, sometimes absent

**Sonic Palette:**
- Vintage Italian/French for music: Pino D'Angiò, Lucio Battisti, Françoise Hardy
- Or: near silence. Cicadas. Distant conversation. Footsteps on stone
- If music: minimal arrangement, space between notes, 60-80 BPM
- Vinyl static as aesthetic choice — the warmth of archive

**Technical Specs:**
- Color: Navy deep shadows, warm stone highlights (#C8B89A), blown skin
- Blue hour timing: 20-40 minutes post-sunset when sky saturates to deep blue-purple
- White balance: set to Tungsten (3200K), creating cool shadows against warm lights
- Exposure: underexpose overall, let highlights blow on skin/light sources
- Duration: 8-12 seconds per clip
- Movement: static camera or imperceptible creep
- Grain: ISO 400-800 natural grain, 35mm aesthetic

**The Rule:** The place owns the moment, not the other way around. Shoot what's already there. Never chase the light.

## THE PALETTE

**Navy Blue as Anchor** — When to invoke the full palette system:

Use this when the entire frame architecture matters, not just individual shots.

**Primary:** Navy blue — not stylized navy. The actual color of deep water.
- #1C2340 (dark navy)
- #0A1628 (navy-black)

**Secondary:** Unbleached stone — the inside walls of old European cities, warm without being yellow.
- #C8B89A

**Dark anchor:** Near-black with texture, never flat.
- #141414
- Always add texture: wool, linen, water, stone — smooth navy reads flat and dies

**Skin:** Overexposed. Blow out the highlights. Let whites go white. Shadows stay dark.

**One accent only:** Either brat acid green (#8ACE00) — a single neon cut — or washed-out terracotta (#B85C38). Choose one and never use both.

**Technical Rule:** 70% of the frame is cool-dark (navy, shadow, deep blue-gray). One warm accent per frame: gold, cream, skin, candlelight.

**What you are NOT using:**
- Teal, sage, dusty rose, warm orange/teal split grade
- Anything from a 2023 moodboard
- The palette must feel found, not designed

**How the Palette Functions:**
- Faces emerge from shadow: side light or backlight, never front-lit against navy
- Texture is mandatory — wool, linen, velvet, water, stone
- Blue hour is your natural navy LUT: 20 minutes post-sunset, sky saturates to deep blue-purple
- Overexposed skin against dark navy creates maximum tension
- One warm accent creates focal point without breaking the cool dominance
